🌱 NGO · Brebes Mengabdi
PAUD AZ-ZAHRA Community School

Founded and scaled under Tim Brebes Mengabdi (TBM) — a community-based NGO serving 250+ households across 3 isolated and underserved villages in Brebes, Indonesia. Self-funded and built from the ground up, PAUD AZ-ZAHRA enrolled 100+ children and trained 9 local women as volunteer teachers, creating a sustainable, community-owned early childhood education model. Advocacy efforts reached the highest local level: direct dialogue with the Brebes Regent secured a committed bridge construction budget in 2016, improving physical access for the community. By 2020, full operational leadership had been transferred to local volunteers — ensuring the program's impact endures beyond the founder.
